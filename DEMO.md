# DEMO: the click path for Caleb (Phase 1)

Start from `supabase db reset`, `npm run seed`, `npm run dev`, signed in at /login as SEED_USER_EMAIL. MAIL_ADAPTER is noop: nothing leaves the machine. One request carries the whole demo.
Replies 1 to 3 and 5 (fixtures/replies/alt-wall-rolling.txt, partial-discount.txt, future-rolling.txt, decline.txt) are pasted on the Offers tab under "Paste text" with the RFQ chosen from the picker. quote.pdf.eml is uploaded under "Upload .eml" with the RFQ left on "Auto-detect from headers": its In-Reply-To is a placeholder id, so auto-match falls back to the picker and you choose Delta Steel Houston.
The channel purchase comes from a fifth line added by hand on the Review tab (C12x30, the item in fixtures/requests/channel-heavier.txt). decline.txt is Delta Steel Fort Worth's answer on that line, a phone quote from Alro Texas is what gets bought, and /history finds C12x30 at the end.

1. /requests/new. Under "Upload an .eml" choose fixtures/requests/mixed-list.eml and press "Create request from email". Notice: /requests/<id> titled "Material list for Job 4471", status New, Customer "Lone Star Fabrication" linked from the sender's domain, the Source pane showing the email exactly as sent, mixed-list.eml under Attachments.

2. /requests/<id> (Review tab). Press "Parse request". Four rows: W12x26, L2x2x1/4, PL3/8, HSS6x6x3/8, each linked under "Catalog item". Click a row: the amber mark in Source covers exactly that line. missing_grade chips on lines 1, 2, 4: blanks stay blank. Type A992 in the Grade cell of line 1 and press Enter: the chip goes. In "Add a line" type C12x30, Qty 6, Unit pcs, Length 40', press "Add line": line 5 links to C12x30 with no span (typed, not parsed). Press "Confirm request": status Reviewed, every row marked confirmed.

3. /requests/<id>/route. Check Delta Steel Houston (preferred) on lines 1 to 5, Bull Moose Gerald on line 4, Morgan Steel Texas on line 2, Triad Metals Richmond on line 1, Delta Steel Fort Worth on line 5. Remove one: uncheck Delta Steel Houston on line 5; the Recipients aside drops Delta to 4 lines. Add one: on line 5 pick "Alro, Alro Texas" under "Add another branch"; it shows checked with the "added by hand" badge (the capability table does not list Alro for channel; the broker knows better). Press "Generate drafts".

4. /requests/<id>/rfqs. Six cards, "0 of 6 sent". Each To is one address at its own branch, Cc blank, Subject "RFQ: Lone Star, 4 items, <date>" for Delta Houston and "1 item" for the rest, the body lists only that card's lines. Edit one Body and click away: "Saving...", then the amber note that it differs from the generated text. Press "Send all (6)": the dialog lists every branch with its address (no cross-vendor recipient), press "Send 6 RFQs". Banner "6 sent.", every card Sent with a Message-ID row, status RFQs sent.

5. /requests/<id>/offers. "Paste text", RFQ "Bull Moose, Bull Moose Gerald", paste alt-wall-rolling.txt, "Ingest reply". Repeat with partial-discount.txt to Morgan Steel Texas and future-rolling.txt to Triad Metals Richmond. Notice: the Bull Moose line is Alternate (size and wall), Offered 8x8x1/2, 64.50 /cwt, Avail. qty blank with missing_qty, Freight "Separate (plus freight, FOB)"; click the row and the sentence lights up under Reply text. Morgan: 60 pcs, 0.98 /ft, Payment terms "1% net 10", Early pay 1% within 10 days. Triad: Rolling, Ready Nov 17, Delivery "five loads, can cut 40'", Price blank with missing_price, Freight Unknown. Make the partial on purpose: edit Morgan's Avail. qty from 60 to 25, Enter. "Mark reviewed" on each card.

6. Same tab. "Upload .eml", choose fixtures/replies/quote.pdf.eml, leave RFQ on "Auto-detect from headers", "Ingest reply". The amber box "The headers match no RFQ sent from this request. Which one is this reply for?" appears: pick "Delta Steel, Delta Steel Houston", press "Attach to this RFQ". Notice: an Email card with "Open the .eml" and delta-quote-4417.pdf links, four Exact lines priced /cwt and /ft, Freight "Included (delivered)", Payment terms "Net 30, 1% 10 days"; click a row: "Selected line is from delta-quote-4417.pdf, page 1." Mark reviewed. Then "Paste text", RFQ "Delta Steel, Delta Steel Fort Worth", paste decline.txt, "Ingest reply": badge Declined, "The vendor declined. No lines.", Notes carry "we don't stock channel".

7. Same tab. Press "Enter a phone or text quote". RFQ "Alro, Alro Texas", Came in by Phone, Received today; tick the Quoted box on line 5, Offered C12x30, Match Exact, Avail. qty 6, Unit pcs, Availability In stock, Price 61.00, Basis /cwt, leave Ready and Delivery blank, press "Save quote". Notice: a Phone card at Confidence 100%; click its row: "This line was entered by hand and has no source span." The "Awaiting a reply" section now reads "Every sent RFQ has a reply." and the status badge reads Comparing.

8. /requests/<id>/compare. Purchase bar: "Awaiting a reply (0)" and "Declined (1)" naming Delta Steel Fort Worth with "we don't stock channel". Line 4 header "2 offers, 1 alternate": the Bull Moose row has the amber border and the "alternate" badge with "size 6x6 to 8x8" and "wall 3/8 to 1/2"; press "Select alternate..." to show the "What changed" dialog, then Cancel. Line 2: Morgan Available "25 pcs" with "15 pcs short" in amber, Extended $490.00, Early pay "if paid in 10 days: $4.90" (a scenario, never folded into the price), Freight "unknown" with the "incomplete" badge. Line 1: the Triad row reads Rolling, Ready Nov 17, 2026, Unit price "unknown", Extended "cannot compute: no price".

9. Same tab. Vendor A for two lines: Delta row on line 1, qty 8, "Select" (footer "Selected 8 of 8 pcs, fully covered"); same on line 3 with 10. Split the third: line 2 Delta row 15, Select, then Morgan row 25, Select: two chips and "Selected 40 of 40 pcs, fully covered" (set a chip to 30 to show the red over-selection warning, then put it back). Line 4: Delta 20, Select. Line 5: Alro 6, Select. Press "Confirm purchase", PO number PO-4471, press "Confirm purchase" in the dialog. Banner "Purchase confirmed: 3 purchases recorded. The request is now sourced.", badge Sourced, chips read-only, "Open purchase history" link.

10. /history. Designation C12x30, press "Search": one row dated today, Alro Texas, C12x30 in bold, 6 pcs, the unit price with its basis, PO-4471, Offered as C12x30. Press Clear, then Free text "c12 x 30": the same row (spaces are ignored). Press "Reply" on the row: the Offers tab opens scrolled to the Alro Phone card (the Delta rows also carry a "Quote file" link to the PDF). End here.
